Method · short version

Only endpoints classified real_commerce (volume ≥ $1k, avg ticket ≥ $0.50, ≥3 distinct buyers) or agent_chain (both buys and sells at non-dust tickets) are ranked. Four noise classes — dust_noise, self_probe, aggregator funnels, low_signal — are excluded by construction. Identity: verified = domain control proven at trust.paycensus.com; attributed = public web discovery, unverified. Full methodology: agentmap.html.
