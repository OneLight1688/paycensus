Gate the policy layer between your agents and their wallets

Enterprises deploying paying agents face the same question every finance team asks: who is allowed to spend, how much, at which merchants, and who approves? Gate is a software policy layer that sits between your agents and their wallets or payment rails. It evaluates every intended payment against budgets, allowlists and approval flows, signs the authorized ones, and records the full intent → authorization → payment → receipt chain for audit.

Gate never touches funds. Keys belong to you — the gateway only evaluates policy and co-signs with keys you control. No custodial risk, no money transmission, no license required.

What it does · four capabilities, one gateway

Budgets per agent
Allocate monthly budgets to each agent, team or department. Spend policies: limits, time windows, per-transaction caps. Enforced before money moves.
Merchant allowlists
Allowlists backed by Trust Rank tiers — real settlement behaviour on stablecoin rails, static reputation heuristics elsewhere. Import a tier, or pin exact merchants.
Approval flows
Thresholds route payments to human approval. Everything below auto-approves; everything above waits. Approvals are signed and logged.
Audit & reports
Full intent → authorization → payment → receipt chain per agent. Export for finance, tax and compliance. Spend reports by agent, merchant and rail.

Governance tiers · who can be governed

TierAgent typeHow it works
NATIVEAgents built with the open SDK / specRegister identity, full governance: budgets, allowlists, approvals, audit.
CONFORMANTThird-party agents speaking open protocols (AP2 mandates, MCP, x402)Pass through the gateway — it reads standard protocol messages regardless of who wrote the agent. Verification is per transaction, not per vendor.
UNGOVERNABLEClosed agents that bypass enterprise controlsCannot be intercepted. Spend telemetry surfaces the gap so finance sees unmapped payments instead of discovering them in an invoice.

Vendors can self-declare conformance with the governance-readiness checklist in the spec. Gate does not certify, rate or resell third-party agents.
