Method

Every selling address is classified from payment-graph behaviour only: real_commerce = volume ≥ $1k, avg ticket ≥ $0.50, ≥3 distinct buyers · agent_chain = the address both buys and sells at non-dust tickets (task delegation) · self_probe = pays itself, or >100 txs to ≤2 counterparties under $100 · dust_noise = avg ticket < $0.01, or a <$0.05 spray to 500+ buyers (airdrop bots, faucets) · aggregator = ≤2 counterparties moving ≥ $10k, or ≥10k txs to ≤5 (gateway funnels) · low_signal = the rest. Attribution (Identity column) comes from public web discovery; claim flow is on-chain-verified domain control.
