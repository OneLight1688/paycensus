The counterparty-trust layer of machine commerce

Machines buying from machines must answer four questions: who are you, may you do this, how do we settle — and should I be dealing with you at all. Identity (ERC-8004), authorization (KYA by Visa/Mastercard/Ant) and settlement (x402/MPP) have protocols. The fourth layer was empty. Trust fills it — with facts, not opinions.

What a verification states — and only this

How it works — three steps, fully automated

  1. Claim your entity — find your address in the directory, start verification with your domain.
  2. Add one DNS record — a one-time TXT token (expires in 48h, single-use nonce).
  3. Instant check — we query 3 independent public resolvers; 2 of 3 must confirm. No human reviews anything. Re-verified every 72h; 3 consecutive failures → public revocation.
