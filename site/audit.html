Proof of Integrity · hash-chained & anchored to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps

SnapshotChain digest (SHA-256)StatusBTC blockVerify

Every day the three public data files are snapshotted, and the snapshot's hash — chained to the previous day's hash — is anchored into the Bitcoin blockchain via OpenTimestamps. This proves, cryptographically, that the published history has never been rewritten after the fact: altering any past day would break every subsequent link. $0 cost, no account, receipts are third-party verifiable with ots verify or verify.opentimestamps.org. Receipts (.ots) are binary proofs — they look empty in a browser; click info for a human-readable summary.
